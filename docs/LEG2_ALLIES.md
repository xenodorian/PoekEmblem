# Leg 2: friendly NPCs as Pokemon

The 8 friendly NPCs are Pokemon with the same treatment as Eirika: a species slot, stats copied from a base species, their FE8 portrait as front and back picture and as a party icon, the base species' cry and footprint, and their own FE8 attack animation in battle.

The learnset of each ally is the base species' learnset with the four signature moves placed at levels 1, 1, 8 and 16 (replacing the base species' own entry for the same move). The species slots are the unused old "?" slots. Species 252 to 259 map to their own cries in `SpeciesToCryId` (`src/pokemon.c`).

## How the attack animation works
`src/ally_attack.c` plays the animation when one of these species attacks on the player side. It hides the portrait, builds the frames out of 64 px sprite parts whose picture data is copied from ROM as each frame starts, waits until the hit frame, then starts the move's own effect, and shows the portrait again only after both the swing and the effect have finished. The portrait, its background copy and after-image clones are never drawn while the swing is on screen. Frame data and timings are generated by `tools/make_ally_attack_gfx.py` from frames made by `tools/render_ally_frames.py`.

Known limits: each frame is cropped to the screen (Tana loses up to 38 px at the top, wide animations are shifted right instead of cropped); bow, staff and dragon breath effects that FE8 draws as separate spell effects are not included, so Innes draws a bow without an arrow and the move's own effect plays instead; one swing per hit for multi-hit moves; the game's BATTLE SCENE: OFF option skips every move animation including the swing.

## Recruiting
`data/scripts/ally_recruit.inc` has `EventScript_RecruitInnes`, `Tana`, `LArachel`, `Dozla`, `Joshua`, `Duessel`, `Cormag` and `Myrrh`. A story event calls one of them. The ally joins at the level of the strongest party member (minimum 5) with all IVs 31, is added to the party if there is room and otherwise sent to the PC, and the event shows the matching message. The party is still 6 Pokemon; the deploy screen and 18 member roster were dropped in favour of double battles (`docs/PARTNER_BATTLES.md`).
