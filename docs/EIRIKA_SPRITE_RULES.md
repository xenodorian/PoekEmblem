# Eirika overworld sprite recolor rules

1. Recolor only, never reshape (hat-to-hair strand pattern approved for the south frame only; show other frames first).
2. Isolate the pixel group first, show the mask, recolor after confirmation.
3. Select by role (position and context), not by color alone. Brown was side hair and eyes. Black was outline and pupils.
4. One shared palette. Hair dark (77,152,165), mid (105,183,204), light (123,198,220). Eyes (30,80,170). Body bands gold (232,196,72)/(190,150,40), red (214,58,48)/(164,36,36), white (250,248,240)/(206,204,214).
5. Bands follow the same top-to-bottom order in every frame, adjusted for pose.
6. Do not touch outline, skin, hands or pupils.
7. Back view has no eyes. East frames mirror west frames, recolor one side only.
8. Show before/after at 8x. Get approval on the first frame of each direction.
9. Work on copies with new filenames. Never overwrite originals.
10. Stay in 4bpp, 16 colors including transparent.
