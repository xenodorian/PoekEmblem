# FireRed Free Space (BPRE USA)

Largest 0xFF runs found:
- 0x71A23C : ~6,184,388 bytes
- 0xEB0B20 : ~1,373,408 bytes
- smaller ones exist

These are ideal for repointing new graphics, text, or scripts when importing FE8 assets.

Note: always verify with a free-space finder after other patches; expansion patches may claim some.
